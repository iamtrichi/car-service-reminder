LOCALIZED FULL DESCRIPTIONS

DO NOT USE IN ANY TITLE
  * "25,000+" in the app NAME. It ranks in the description but Google Play's
    app-name policy discourages promotional figures, and a name change is
    harder to revert than description text. It is used in short descriptions
    and full descriptions only.

  * The "Alt" names (Car Cost & Service Log, My Car Service Book, etc.) trade
    brand consistency for keyword reach. If you pick one, apply it to all five
    locales at the same time so the brand name does not fork per market.

  * "100% offline" as a headline claim — two features (Pexels photo search,
    IP currency detection) do use the network. ALT C still leads with offline
    because it is a genuine and prominent product truth, and it states the
    mechanism ("data is stored locally on your device") rather than an absolute.
  * Cost per km — not implemented in statsService, so it is never promised.
