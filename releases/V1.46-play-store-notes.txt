<en-US>
🚗 V1.46 Release
Features included in this release:
• Added interactive first-run walkthrough tour (23 steps) guiding you through adding a vehicle, documents, fuel logging, and expenses
• Added Replay App Tour option in Settings to restart the walkthrough anytime
• Updated Opel engine data (deduplicated and merged model entries)
• Fixed fuel form odometer validation to allow 0 km for brand-new cars
